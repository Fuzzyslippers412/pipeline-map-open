## Publisher material

Source publishers retain any rights they hold in their original material. The project makes no license grant on their behalf. Individual facts and the expression or arrangement of a source table have different legal treatment. [U.S. Copyright Office guidance on databases](https://www.copyright.gov/register/tx-databases.html)
