# Workplace suggestion sources

These are directory matches for the starting point of a trace. A site can appear in this directory without a workplace-history record, an opening, or an employment relationship with the reader. Directory entries never verify a reader's employer, role, hiring process or training. Sources have different coverage and editions. An organization may have changed names or structure since its edition.

- **Public schools and education agencies.** The public-domain [NCES EDGE public school locations, 2024–25](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/public-school-locations-2024-25) and the companion [NCES EDGE agency locations](https://nces.ed.gov/programs/edge/Geographic/SchoolLocations) give site names and public locations. Each school joins its reported education agency by exact LEAID. Agencies include regular districts, charter operators and other administrative units. This is a reported agency relationship, not a legal ownership assertion. Both the school and agency office can be selected under their own published names. Official labels occasionally truncate longer names.
- **Colleges and universities.** [NCES IPEDS HD2024 directory](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/DataFiles.aspx?year=2024). The loader includes CYACTIVE institutions, their reported city/state, and published alias strings. F1SYSNAM supplies the named multi-institution or multi-campus organization only where the reporting fields identify one. Its relationship is labeled as an education system or organization. A shared OPEID prefix or similar name does not establish a parent.
- **Hospitals and community health sites.** HRSA publishes [CMS hospitals](https://gisportal.hrsa.gov/server/rest/services/HealthCareFacilities/CMSApprovedFacilities_FS/MapServer/0), [critical-access hospitals](https://gisportal.hrsa.gov/server/rest/services/HealthCareFacilities/CMSApprovedFacilities_FS/MapServer/1), and [health care service delivery sites](https://gisportal.hrsa.gov/server/rest/services/HealthCareFacilities/PrimaryHealthCareFacilities_FS/MapServer/0). Hospital layers overlap; their Medicare provider numbers are deduplicated. Active community health sites can show the HRSA-named **program grantee**. That agency may differ from the site's corporate owner or the employer named on a pay stub. Hospital-only records without a separately sourced relationship omit the parent. These sources do not enumerate all workplaces or establish that a historic provider remains open today.
- **SEC registrants.** The [SEC's company-ticker/exchange file](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-search-assistance/accessing-edgar-data) supplies publicly reported company names and ticker aliases. Entries are grouped by Central Index Key, with at least one nonempty exchange association. The SEC does not guarantee complete or current coverage. Registrants include international companies and investment entities, and this register does not identify an employee's branch or parent company.
- **Publisher-verified examples.** A small set of employer-published campus and facility facts supplements the registers. Each row cites its source in the `sources` map. For example, Howard's financial statements describe [Howard University Hospital as an operating segment of Howard University](https://howard.edu/sites/home.howard.edu/files/2025-06/Howard%20University%20Hospital%20FS%20Dec%2031%202024.pdf). The Department of Veterans Affairs, Emory Healthcare, Grady Health and corporate-campus publishers identify their own sites. A campus organization, health system and legal parent are labeled separately.
- **Reviewed historical workplaces.** Additional dated rows carry their own `sourceUrl` and review date. The November 4 expansion adds New York City's Law Department, the NAACP Legal Defense and Educational Fund, and Champion's Memphis pharmacy. A historical business or appointment record does not verify current operation, staffing or a reader's employment. University-wide directory identities retain the department or faculty scope of each historical record; they do not identify a clinical site. Preserve these manually reviewed additions when refreshing the source snapshots.

The two committed JSON files contain only institutional IDs, names, alternate names, public city/state, organization relationship (if documented), classification and source metadata. Personal contact fields, street addresses and source workbooks are not redistributed. Rights in third-party names, marks and original publications remain with their holders. References describe the government data products and original publisher facts; the project’s code and data licenses do not grant rights in publishers' source material.

## Refreshing the local directory

Run `python3 loaders/build-workplace-directory.py --cache /path/outside/repo`. Only Python's standard library is needed. Source snapshots are cached outside the project. Add `--refresh` to fetch a new edition of mutable feeds. Both JSON outputs are validated before replacement. A failed or partial source refresh must be investigated before publishing. Coordinates, working hours, current vacancy and personal data do not determine matching or ranking.

`workplace-directory.json` includes colleges, companies, hospitals, health sites and individually cited examples. `workplace-schools.json` is a separate, nationally scoped school/agency source shard. Each file contains `asOf`, its `sources` map and `records`. The as-of date reflects source-file retrieval; the source map specifies the survey edition when the publisher supplies one. Case-only cleanup improves legibility without guessing organizational relationships. Institutions sharing a display name retain distinct source IDs where source entities differ.
