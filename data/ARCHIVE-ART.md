# Door Notes archival photographs

## Volunteer aide training at Freedmen’s Hospital

Roger Smith of the U.S. Office of War Information photographed instructor Thelma Harris and volunteer nurse’s aide Maxine Jackson during a lesson at Freedmen’s Hospital in Washington, D.C., in February 1943. The Library of Congress caption identifies the lesson as filling an ice cap. The photograph documents this particular training session. Jackson’s role in this record is volunteer aide.

The source is the [Library of Congress catalogue record, LCCN 2017696521](https://www.loc.gov/item/2017696521/). The negative is LC-USE6-D-009006. The digital identifier is fsa 8b06029. Credit to the Library of Congress, Prints & Photographs Division, Farm Security Administration/Office of War Information Black-and-White Negatives.

The catalogue’s Rights & Access section identifies this collection as public domain and free to reuse. See also the Library’s [rights and restrictions information](https://www.loc.gov/rr/print/res/071_fsab.html).
