# Job-title suggestions

The local title directory is derived from the [O*NET® 31.0 Database](https://www.onetcenter.org/database.html) by the U.S. Department of Labor, Employment and Training Administration (USDOL/ETA). The source files are the [Job Titles CSV](https://www.onetcenter.org/dl_files/database/db_31_0_csv/job_titles.csv) and [Sample of Reported Titles CSV](https://www.onetcenter.org/dl_files/database/db_31_0_csv/sample_of_reported_titles.csv). The source data are available under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). [Database license and attribution requirements](https://www.onetcenter.org/license_db.html).

Door Notes retained job titles, short titles and reported titles that fit the entry field, normalized whitespace, and deduplicated spellings case-insensitively. Its search combines these strings with its own profession names, common titles and specialties. The project assigns its own reviewed-field and coverage labels. A matching string is a suggestion for what to enter; it is not evidence of that employer's hiring or of a personal pathway. USDOL/ETA has not approved, endorsed, or tested these modifications. O*NET® is a trademark of USDOL/ETA.

The release's exact row counts, checksums, source URLs and field selection are in [job-title-source.json](../public/data/job-title-source.json). Run `python3 loaders/build-job-title-directory.py` to reproduce the local [job-titles.json](../public/data/job-titles.json) from the two release files. The suggestions use local data and do not send typed job titles to USDOL/ETA.
